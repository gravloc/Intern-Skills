name: research-brief
description: Produce a short, sourced research brief on a market, standard, competitor, technology or event. Use for any "find out about X" request.
---
## Steps
1. Restate the question and what decision it feeds.
2. Search broadly, then read the best 4-8 sources. Prefer official and
   primary sources; note the date of each.
3. Write the one-page brief: Answer in 3 bullets, evidence table
   (claim | source URL | date | Confirmed/Likely/Unverified), conflicting
   information, gaps, recommended next step.
4. Never present a guess as a fact. If sources disagree, show both.
5. Paraphrase; do not copy text or images from sources.
6. Save in Drive/Notion and link it in Linear.
