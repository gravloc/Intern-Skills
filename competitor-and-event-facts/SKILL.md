name: competitor-and-event-facts
description: Collect factual data on competitors (satsearch, satbase and others) or on conferences (SmallSat, IAC, Space Tech Expo). Use for fact sheets that Marketing, CFO or the CEO will rely on.
---
## Steps
1. Browse public pages only. No logins, no scraping of personal data.
2. Competitors: features, categories, supplier counts if stated, pricing if
   public, recent announcements. Event info: dates, location, ticket and
   booth cost if published, audience, deadlines.
3. Record every fact with source URL and date checked. Mark estimates
   and opinions separately.
4. Put results in the shared fact-sheet Sheet as a new dated tab.
5. Flag material changes to the requester.
