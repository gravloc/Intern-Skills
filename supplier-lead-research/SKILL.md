name: supplier-lead-research
description: Research potential founding suppliers (manufacturers and authorised distributors of space components) for the Business Developer. Use for building or extending the supplier lead list.
---
## Steps
1. Confirm scope: region (US, EU, Japan, India), component category, how
   many leads, qualification criteria.
2. Search public sources in the browser: company sites, association member
   directories, conference exhibitor lists, trade press, public certifier
   directories.
3. For each company fill the standard lead columns. Record manufacturer vs
   distributor, certifications seen and where, and a public business
   contact page URL (no personal emails or phone numbers of individuals).
4. Mark certifications and heritage claims Unverified unless confirmed on
   an issuer page.
5. Skip duplicates (check the master tracker). Flag any company that
   looks export-sensitive (defence-focused ITAR text) for the founder.
6. Do not contact anyone. Hand the list to the Business Developer.
