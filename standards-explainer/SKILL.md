## Steps
1. Use official or standards-body pages and reputable technical sources.
2. Write: what it is, who uses it, why it matters to buyers and suppliers,
   how it appears on datasheets, common confusions.
3. Never quote long passages of copyrighted standards; summarise and link.
4. Mark anything uncertain [VERIFY]. Technical correctness will be checked
   by the CTO or space advisor before anything is published externally.
5. Do not give compliance or legal conclusions.
