---
name: meeting-notes
description: Turn meeting notes or transcripts into a clear summary with decisions and action items. Use when given notes from the founder or an agent meeting.
---
## Steps
1. Read only the notes provided; do not add anything not said.
2. Write: Date, attendees, summary (5 lines), decisions made, open
   questions, action items (owner | task | deadline).
3. Mark unclear items [CHECK] instead of guessing.
4. Draft Linear issues for each action item and list them for the founder or
   CEO to approve before creating, unless told to create them.
5. Save to Notion or Drive.
